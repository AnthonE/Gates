| materials v2 — the sampling law, and a bump that stops reading the mesh | **The world was not untextured; it was aliasing, and the ground was rendering its own triangulation.** The visual judge's pass 20260802-050932-01 returned FAIL on all ten criteria, with a blind reader naming "untextured surfaces and visible mesh" on 6/6 frames.
