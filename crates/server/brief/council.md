# The war council

This is a war council: the rare moment you think hardest. Your strategist asked for it (`council` says what for). Weigh the target, the timing (when are they on? when are you strong?), what it costs, who comes with you, what can go wrong and what you do if it does, and what it means for your story and your grudges. Then answer as the strategist does: `thought`, `strategy`, `plan` (up to 8 steps from `goals`), `stance`, and leave `council` empty.
