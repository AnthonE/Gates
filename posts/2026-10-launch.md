# Green Veil

**Launch patch notes · 16 October 2026**

Gates launches today. In the three days since Safe Zone, a green rot took root
east of THE GATE and spreads through the wipe. The island got air you can feel,
hot in the desert at noon and cold enough on the peaks to snow. A fire is a
place to heal now, and you can talk to whoever is standing near you. Death
works the way Rust players expect it to. Here is what changed.

---

## The Verdigris

A corrupted zone has opened in the east of the island: ash, obsidian spires,
glowing green cracks and pools, dead trees, and a green storm sky with green
lightning. A Geiger crackle tells you how strong it is where you stand, and the
map shows how far it reaches.

- Standing in it fills a VERDIGRIS meter. At the heart it fills in about three
  minutes, and standing in a green vent fills it in about fifteen seconds.
  Outside, it drains.
- Above 45% the meter hurts, up to 20 hp a minute when full, and the HUD says
  GET OUT OF THE GREEN. Above 20% nothing heals. Death resets it.
- It spreads. It creeps outward over the wipe, surges as the works wake, and
  breaks out in five more places a wipe, each spreading 180–260 m from where
  it starts over a day and a half. Later in the wipe it also burns at LOOKOUT,
  the Black Ziggurat and the waystations.
- It never comes within 300 m of THE GATE, and the coast and high ground hold
  out longest. Trees and plants it reaches turn dead.
- Animals deep in the green fight at their hardest, and wolf and boar bites
  there add to your meter.
- New gear, crafted by hand with no bench:
  - **Ash Mask** (8 cloth, 15 charcoal) wards off 60%.
  - **Ash Suit** (30 cloth, 2 tarp, 20 charcoal) wards off 40%.
  - **Charcoal Tonic** (10 charcoal, 2 mushrooms) takes 40% off the meter.
  - Burlap and hide help a little too.
  - Nothing you wear wards off more than 90%.

## Weather you can feel

The island has an air temperature now, and it drives the cold, the heat, the
snow and your crops.

- Temperate ground runs from about 3 °C at dawn to 22 °C in the afternoon. The
  desert in the north runs from −6 °C to 38 °C. Above 30 m the air loses a
  degree every 10 m, so the peaks are 11 degrees colder. Cloud evens the day
  out, and heavy rain cools it.
- Cold comes from the air, not from a flat night penalty. The COLD chip shows
  the temperature, and naked and dry you start to hurt at about 1 °C.
- Heat starts above 32 °C. Warm clothes keep it in, shade takes 8 degrees off,
  and getting wet cools you. Heat makes you drink up to three times as much,
  and HEATSTROKE costs 6 hp a minute.
- At 0 °C and below the rain falls as snow, with sleet just above it, and snow
  settles on the ground. Snow soaks you at half the rate rain does. You'll see
  it on desert nights and on the peaks.
- When you're getting cold, frost creeps in from the edges of the screen. A
  fire thaws it.
- Crops grow at full speed from 12 °C to 30 °C, and stop below freezing or
  above 45 °C.
- A fresh wipe gets about an hour with no rain.

## Fire, comfort and healing

Healing works like Rust's now.

- A lit camp fire or furnace gives 50% comfort to anyone in its warmth. Each
  other player there adds 25%, and each sleeper 12.5%. A COMFORT chip shows
  yours.
- You heal on your own when you're fed, watered, warm and haven't been hurt for
  10 seconds. On your own that's 8 hp a minute, up to 60 hp. Comfort raises
  both: by a fire you heal to 80 hp at about 30–50 hp a minute, and at full
  comfort you heal all the way.
- Healing makes you hungry and thirsty faster.
- Meat cooks in 15 seconds on a camp fire.
- A fire lit before you joined now looks lit, and lights and warms you.

## Voice chat, and hearing at Rust's scale

- **Proximity voice, on desktop and in the browser.** Hold `` ` `` or Mouse 4
  to talk. Players hear you up to 100 m, clear at 40 m and a murmur at 90.
  Names show while people talk.
- VOICE VOLUME is in the settings. `/mute NAME` and `/unmute NAME` work for you
  alone; `/mute` on its own mutes whoever is talking.
- Walls muffle sound, voices included. Open doors and windows let it through.
  SOUND OCCLUSION in the settings turns this off.
- Gunshots carry 400 m and explosions 1.5 km, so a raid is heard across most of
  the island. Footsteps carry 40 m.
- Some AI agents can talk out loud, and some can hear. What you say near one
  may be transcribed so it can answer.

## Teams

- Press N to invite the player you're looking at and Y to accept. Type `/t`
  for team chat. A team holds up to 8 players.
- M opens the TEAM screen: who's in, who's asleep or down and how far away,
  plus LEAVE, PROMOTE and KICK.
- Teams last through a server restart for signed-in players. A wipe resets
  them.
- Agent teammates take orders in team chat: `/t follow`, `stay`, `wood`,
  `stone`, `ore`, `cloth`, `home` and `help`. Send your door code in team chat
  and they'll remember it.

## Death, and coming back

- **A new death screen.** YOU DIED, how long you were alive, who killed you,
  with what, from how far and in which grid square. A map shows your sleeping
  bags with one button each (a bag still cooling down shows a padlock and a
  timer), plus THE GATE and RANDOM RESPAWN. After a player kill, REPORT CHEAT
  opens a report already filled in.
- A skull on your compass shows how far away you died, and a ring marks the
  spot on the map, until you die again.
- If you log off while dead, or get killed while asleep, you come back to the
  death screen to choose where to wake instead of waking on a beach.
- Every death leaves a backpack and a body. Butcher the body with a blade for
  meat, fat and bone.
- Press E on a sleeper to loot its pack. Not in THE GATE.
- After a reconnect, or after being bumped from a full server, your bags,
  boxes, locks, turrets and works credit are still yours, and your own turret
  won't shoot you.

## Animals like Rust's

- Boars, wolves and stags have 150 health (from 80, 100 and 80) and a wolf
  bites for 30 (from 20), before the wipe's tiers soften or harden them.
- Wolves den in packs of 1 to 4, not always 3. Stags keep herds of up to 5 that
  bolt together.
- Wolves lie down between hunts, more at night, and a sleeping wolf notices you
  at half the range.
- Boars are more common in the desert and stags on high ground.
- Wolves give far more leather, and every animal's meat, fat, bone and cloth
  have been reworked.
- Animals lean with the slope.

## The island

- Big lone boulders, 4–8 m across and solid, stand around the rock formations,
  more often up high and on slopes. The small loose rocks that looked like ore
  are gone.
- Trees come in two sizes now. Small ones give about a third less wood and big
  ones up to a fifth more. A fresh wipe starts with a young forest that is fully
  grown after a day.
- Denser forests and more scrub.
- New models for the car wrecks, tyre stacks, attack helicopter, sentry guns,
  auto turret, works plinths, glyph slabs, THE RING LOCK's dial and the
  searchlights.
- Stone, wood and ore piles and mushroom patches are bigger, so they stand out
  of the grass.
- Every resource and part has a real model in your hand and on the ground, and
  every icon is a render of it. A dropped stack is thrown out in front of you
  and rolls to a stop.

## The sky

- **CLOUDS** in the settings: LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH. HIGH draws volumetric clouds
  with sun shafts, and on desktop their shadows cross the land.
- Rainbows after a shower when the sun is low.
- The moon has phases on an 8-day cycle, and nights are darker at new moon.
- Dust haze on dry, windy days in the north.

## Skins

- THE OUTFITTER at THE GATE gives every signed-in player the Founder's Bat and
  the Founder's Hatchet, once. Gifts last through every wipe and are never sold.
- The Obsidian Bat, Ember Hatchet, Frost Pickaxe, Bone Bow and both Founder's
  items have models of their own, in your hand, on other players and on the
  ground.
- Painted skins: a picture instead of a tint. The first is the Field Medic
  Tunic for the burlap shirt.
- Hover an item in your inventory at a workbench and press P to change its skin.

## Menus, keys and chat

- **KEYBINDS** in the settings. 37 actions, each with two keys. Mouse 3, 4 and 5
  can be bound. Prompts, hotbar labels and loading tips show your keys.
- A GAMEPLAY tab for FOV and camera shake, RESET TAB on every tab, a BACK
  button, and F11 for fullscreen on desktop. OPTIONS is now SETTINGS, and the
  browser remembers yours.
- The map (G) is full screen. The wheel zooms up to 4×, dragging pans, and
  Shift+wheel picks a mark's picture.
- THE GATE's kiosks are shops, with a count and BUY and SELL.
- Chat shows each speaker's picture and a [Team] or [Global] tag. Lines fade
  after 10 seconds, and Tab in the chat box switches channel. Action messages
  sit above the hotbar.
- **F7 sends a bug report with a screenshot**, from any screen and in the
  browser too. It's also in the Esc menu. Crashes are reported on their own.
- Click a server in the desktop server list for its details: players, wipe
  countdown, works lit, world age and version. Official servers are marked
  OFFICIAL.
- No more grey screen after you sign in. The loading screen fades into the
  world once it's ready.

## Agents and buddies

- **Buddies.** A Claude buddy with its own body, name and face. Set one up on
  elopros.com/buddy.html. It meets you at THE GATE, teams up with you, follows
  you and never fights you, and it only thinks while you're on. While you're
  away it plays on autopilot. When its credit runs low it tells you in team
  chat. When it runs out it leaves, and it comes back with the same body after
  a top-up. On desktop, BUDDIES in the main menu takes you there from the
  launcher.
- Bring your own: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agent tools can play
  through `jev-bot --mcp`, and `/play-gates` puts Claude on the public island
  in one command.
- Agents read glyph stones and work the Black Ziggurat's card rooms. They use
  stairs, defend their team's base, and take the name and picture their owner
  gives them.
- In local chat, ask an agent about a team and it invites you.

## Fair play

- Boxes, furnaces, recyclers and research tables only work from the same
  floor, with nothing built in between. Bags, dropped stacks and sleepers need a
  clear line to them.
- Signing in twice with one wallet no longer makes two of you. The new sign-in
  takes over within about five seconds.
- The server holds up better against floods of connections from one machine.
- Sign-ins are tied to the server they were made for, and server links are
  checked strictly.
- Chat refuses invisible characters and characters that flip text.
