# Cold Snap

**Patch notes · 30 October 2026**

Since Full Auto, the island got colder. The far south is arctic now, and the
high peaks keep their snow all year. Bullets fly and drop instead of landing
the instant you fire, every gun takes attachments, and there are eight new
kinds of ammo. Bases can run on power, and the auto turret fires whatever gun
you give it. You can sign in with your Elo Pros account, and F9 turns the last
30 seconds into a video. This update comes with a full wipe, blueprints
included. Here is what changed.

---

## The cold

- **An arctic south.** A band of snow runs across the far south of the
  island, opposite the desert, with tundra along its edge. The air there sits
  between -18 and -4 °C. Nobody wakes up on its beaches.
- **Snowcaps.** Anything above a snowline around 98 m is snow all year.
- Snow ground has conifers, metal-heavy rocks and no grass, ferns or moss.
  Wolves and stags live there.
- Snow falling in the arctic settles there. Footsteps and landings crunch and
  kick up a puff. Crickets go quiet on snow, in freezing air and in the
  Verdigris.
- The map shows the snow.
- **Hide Hood.** 10 leather, no bench. 6% protection, 20% against the cold
  and 5% against the Verdigris, with no movement cost.

## Bullets fly

- Every round is a projectile now. It takes time to arrive and drops over
  distance. 5.56 leaves the barrel at 375 m/s, pistol rounds at 300 and shells
  at 225.
- The Bolt Action Rifle fires at 1.75× a round's speed and the Custom SMG at
  0.8×.
- Each gun fires at its exact rate. The Thompson is 462 rounds a minute.
- The crosshair spreads with the cone, and crouching halves the kick.
- A gun keeps its own rounds and attachments when you move it, drop it, loot
  it or hand it over. Carry two guns and the HUD counts each one's rounds.

| Muzzle Boost | muzzle | fires 10% faster; 90% damage | 100 frags, 1 metal pipe (workbench 1) |

- One attachment per slot. Rifles and SMGs take all of them. The Bolt Action
  Rifle takes everything but the Muzzle Boost and Extended Magazine. The
  Semi-Automatic Pistol takes everything but the scope. The Revolver takes a
  sight, a silencer, a laser or a light, and the Python and Pump Shotgun take
  a sight, a laser or a light.
- Every attachment is a Blueprint on the tech tree. The blue room and the
  sanctum drop them too.

## Power

- **Wire Tool** (25 frags, 1 rope). Run cables up to 30 m between parts.
- **Small Generator** (40 power). Burns one low grade fuel every 14 seconds.
- **Large Solar Panel** (20 power). Follows the sun, so it gives nothing at
  night.
- **Small Battery** (15 out, stores 400) and **Large Battery** (100 out,
  stores 24,000). A battery drains only what is drawn from it.
- **Switch**, **Splitter** and **Root Combiner** (1 each) and the
  **Ceiling Light** (2).
- The grid works out every half second who gets power. A wire that would
  make a loop is refused. Cables are drawn, and lamps light.
- Each player can place a limited number of grid parts.

## Auto turret

- **The turret fires the gun you give it.** Put a gun in its weapon slot and
  that gun's ammo in the six slots beside it. It fires at the gun's own rate
  and reloads between magazines, for 60% of the gun's damage, out to 30 m.
- It beeps for a second before it opens up.
- **It needs 10 power.** The turret panel says what reaches it.
- **Authorize list.** Whoever places a turret is on its list, and the turret
  never fires at anyone on it. Anyone with building privilege can authorize
  themselves or clear the list. Anyone can take themselves off.
- **Attack all or peacekeeper.** A new turret attacks all: it shoots anyone
  off its list. The PEACEKEEPER switch on its panel, for anyone with building
  privilege, makes it shoot only people who are hostile: anyone who attacked
  a player in the last five minutes, or hit a turret.
- The turret has a new model, and its recipe is 300 frags, 4 gears and 2
  tech trash. It no longer takes a revolver.

- **The Pump Rug**, a look for the Abyss Rug. THE OUTFITTER sells it for 100

## Clips and watching

- **F9 saves a clip of the last 30 seconds.** In the browser it becomes an MP4,
  tall or wide, with a title over the first seconds and an end card with your
  link. On desktop the clip goes in your screenshots folder, and
  `gates --clip` turns it into a video.
- **WATCH AN AGENT** in the menu puts you in a spectator's seat on the island
  you pick.
- Streamers: `?obs=1` on the play page takes the watch seat without a click
  and keeps the menu and its music off stream. `?streamer=1`, the Options
  switch and elopros.com's streamer mode all turn streamer mode on.

## Feel

- You walk at 2.8 m/s.
- The default mouse speed is the usual shooter turn per inch, and the browser
  reads raw mouse movement like the desktop does.
- A pig runs at 80% of your sprint, so you can run one down. A wolf runs at
  105%, so you can't outrun it.
- 34 new models replace placeholder shapes: the stump, torch, revolver,
  crossbow, stone spear, clothing, parts, seeds and arrows. Arrows in the world
  look like their item, and the bat is high detail.

## Prices

- BUILDERS at THE GATE costs more, so buying no longer beats gathering. The
  Hearth is 50 junk (was 10), 500 wood 30 (was 15) and 500 stone 30 (was 20).

## Agents

- Bots gather what their base needs, not whatever is in view, and stop
  farming once their stores are full. They smelt what their box holds, sell
  surplus for junk, wrap bleeds, and warm up by a fire when they're freezing,
  just not inside the Verdigris.
- When the server refuses a bot's work, the bot says why.
- A bot that gathered until the nodes ran out counts the job done instead of
  failed.
- A bot that can't hand over because its founder is behind walls or a shut
  door, or that finds a door on its way out won't open, says so instead of
  blaming a lock or the server.
- `jev-bot --journal` writes down what an agent did and why.

## Fair play

- The server now flags snap hits, unusual headshot rates and tracking
  through walls for review. Hitting someone who is already down doesn't count
  toward the rates.
