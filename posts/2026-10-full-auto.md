# Full Auto

**Patch notes · 22 October 2026**

Since Green Veil, Gates got eight new guns, rockets and C4, and a tech tree
that goes much further. You can build at any angle, farm, swim, bleed, and
dress for the cold in seven slots. THE GATE grew into a sandstone city with a
casino in the middle, and your team can follow the base from a phone. Loot,
food, water and melee were all reworked. This update comes with a wipe. Here
is what changed.

---

## Guns

Eight new guns join the revolver. Every gun has an aim cone, bloom from
sustained fire, recoil you pull down, and damage that falls off with range.
Aiming down the sights tightens the cone and halves the kick.

| Gun | Damage | Rounds | Fire | Ammo | Hits to kill, unarmoured |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assault Rifle | 50 | 30 | full auto, 450/min | 5.56 | 2 |
| Semi-Automatic Rifle | 40 | 16 | semi | 5.56 | 3 |
| Bolt Action Rifle | 80 | 4 | bolt, pin-point when aimed | 5.56 | 2 |
| Thompson | 38 | 20 | full auto, 450/min | pistol | 3 |
| Custom SMG | 30 | 30 | full auto, 600/min | pistol | 4 |
| Python Revolver | 55 | 6 | semi | pistol | 2 |
| Semi-Automatic Pistol | 40 | 10 | semi | pistol | 3 |
| Revolver | 35 | 8 | semi | pistol | 3 |
| Pump Shotgun | 15 pellets × 14 | 8 | pump | 12 gauge buckshot | 1 shell |

- Every gun is a Blueprint: research it at a table, or unlock it on the tech
  tree. They're crafted from frags, pipes, springs and a gun body. The Black
  Ziggurat's blue room can drop a Python, and the sanctum drops rifles,
  Thompsons, shotguns, rockets and C4.
- 5.56 rifle ammo (3 for 10 frags and 5 gunpowder) and 12 gauge buckshot (2
  for 5 frags and 10 gunpowder) are new.
- Each gun family reloads with its own sound, and you hear other players
  reload theirs. Far gunfire sounds different for rifles, SMGs, pistols,
  pumps and bolt rifles, so you can tell a fight across the valley by ear.
- An empty gun clicks.
- Click any weapon in your inventory for its card: what one hit does, and a
  shotgun's damage × pellets.

## Raiding

- **Rocket Launcher.** A rocket flies 30 m a second, drops a little, and
  bursts 3 m wide with 275 damage. Against walls it hits as hard as a satchel.
- **Timed Explosive Charge (C4).** Ten-second fuse, 3 m blast, never a dud,
  and more than twice a satchel's damage to a building.
- **Beancan Grenade.** Cheap (60 gunpowder, 20 frags), a four-second fuse
  and a 2 m blast. About one in seven is a dud.
- **Barbed Wooden Barricade** (400 hp) and **Floor Spikes** cut anyone who
  touches them.
- You can only plant a charge on what you can see: the first wall in your
  line of sight, or a box or cupboard in clear view. No more planting through
  the outer wall onto the cupboard behind it.

## Build at any angle

- **Freeform building.** A foundation placed on open ground starts its own
  building, turned to face wherever you're looking. Any base, at any angle.
- Triangles are equilateral now.
- Walls, floors, stairs, doors, windows, deployables and upkeep all work on a
  turned building the same as a square one.
- The placement ghost asks the server the same question the server asks, so
  green means it will place, and a placed piece shows up at once.
- A new build wheel: the chosen shape in green, a big icon, its name, a line
  on what it is and its price, and 21 new shape icons.
- Q and E pick a building skin on the hammer's Upgrade.
- The hearth is as tall as a player now: 1.54 × 1.8 × 0.92 m.
- Stone upgrades thud into place, metal goes on with two hammer blows, and
  repairs hammer.

## Farming

- **A planter box has six beds**, and each seed is one plant. Plants grow
  through stages, ripen, and wither if you leave them. Water them from a
  waterskin or let the rain do it. A plant's health is the worst of its
  water, its soil and the air.
- **Sow straight into the ground** in open meadow or forest. The soil sets
  how fast it grows.
- **Genes.** Seeds carry genes. Plants in a bed crossbreed once, and C cuts a
  clone that keeps its parent's genes.
- **Water Catcher** (100 wood, 1 tarp): banks the rain, fills a skin, and
  waters whatever grows within 3 m.
- **New crops:** potatoes, pumpkins and berry bushes. Eating a corn cob, a
  potato or a pumpkin leaves you its seed, and berries do a quarter of the
  time.
- Wild corn and pumpkins grow by the lakes.
- RATIONS sells four potatoes for 3 JUNK.

## Water

- **Swimming.** Water deeper than 1.15 m floats you. Crouch dives, jump rises,
  and you steer where you look. You hold your breath for 10 seconds and then
  drown. No guns, bows, charges or building while you swim.
- Foundations stand in up to 2 m of water. Other deployables need dry land.
- **Right mouse pours** a full skin or jug. Fresh water puts out fires and
  waters planters. Salt water dries them out.
- Fresh water gives 30 water. The sea gives 15 and costs 3 hp.
- The **Salt Waterskin** fills at the sea and boils fresh on a camp fire in 30
  seconds. The **Water Jug** (25 frags) carries two skins of lake water.
- A new sea: the swell moves under you, breakers roll in, rain rings the
  surface, swimmers leave wakes, and from underwater you see the sky through
  a window above you. Lakes are murkier than the sea.

## Clothing, bleeding and teas

- **Seven clothing slots**: head, face, shirt, body, legs, hands
  and feet.
- **Fourteen new clothes:** Burlap Trousers, Burlap Shoes, Burlap Gloves, Hide
  Pants, Hide Boots, Hide Vest, Leather Gloves, Hoodie, Pants, Jacket, Snow
  Jacket, Bandana, Improvised Balaclava and Beenie Hat. The Snow Jacket is the
  warmest thing on the island and keeps out 90% of the cold.
- **Bleeding.** A hit opens a wound worth a fifth of its damage, which bleeds
  20 hp a minute until it has taken that much. A BLEEDING chip shows it, and
  the death screen says if you bled out.
- **Bandages** are held now. Click to wrap one on over 4 seconds, and switch
  away to cancel. A bandage stops 50 bleeding and a medkit 100.
- **Teas**, each worth 30 water:

| Tea | Recipe | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Warming Tea | 4 berries, 2 animal fat | +5 °C for 20 minutes |
| Strong Warming Tea | 2 warming teas, 2 mushrooms | +10 °C for 30 minutes, and the cold can't hurt you |
| Cooling Tea | 3 cactus flesh, 2 berries | −10 °C for 20 minutes |
| Warding Tea | 10 charcoal, 2 mushrooms, 2 berries | keeps out 15% more of the Verdigris for 30 minutes |
| Healing Tea | 3 berries, 2 mushrooms | 30 hp over 30 seconds |

## A bigger tech tree

The tech tree is a full-screen page now: a LEVEL tab for
each bench, your JUNK count, and an item rail with UNLOCK. Unlocking a node
buys the whole path to it.

Sixteen new items to unlock:

- **Melee:** Salvaged Hammer, Salvaged Sword, Mace, Salvaged Cleaver,
  Longsword, Salvaged Axe and Salvaged Icepick. Of the new blades, the
  longsword hits hardest and reaches furthest.
- **Armour:** Coffee Can Helmet, Tank Top and Boots.
- **Hazmat Suit:** a waterproof, full-body piece. It takes 25% off hits, keeps
  out half the cold and wards off half the Verdigris, but you can't wear a
  helmet or boots with it.
- The beancan, the barbed barricade and the floor spikes.

Benches and the research table stand on bare ground now.

## Melee lands where it's drawn

- A swing lands 133 ms after you click, when the animation strikes, so you
  see the blow land when it lands. You can read a swing coming and step out of
  it.
- Every weapon reaches as far as its model does: the bat 1.35 m, hatchets
  0.95 m, pickaxes about 1.1 m, the torch 0.9 m, spears 1.75–1.9 m. A spear
  wins the fight it starts.
- Lag compensation rewinds to exactly what you saw, up to 500 ms back, so
  shots and swings on a high ping land where they looked like they would.
- **Punching.** An empty hand throws a weak punch (5 damage) that costs you
  2 hp.
- No grunt when you're hurt. A headshot plays a crunch, and the
  shooter hears it too.
- Wolves yelp, boars squeal and stags bleat when you hit them, and each cries
  out when it dies.

## THE GATE

- **A city now.** THE GATE is a 92 m sandstone gate with a 41 m ring, and the
  containers around it are the ancient city's blocks.
- **The casino.** A Big Wheel and slot machines, staked in JUNK, under a
  timber-trussed roof that keeps the rain off.
  - The wheel has 25 segments: twelve 1s, six 3s, four 5s, two 10s and one
    20. A win pays your stake times the number, plus your stake back. Bets
    are open for 45 seconds, then it spins for 15, up to 100 JUNK a colour.
  - Slots cost 2 JUNK a line, one to five lines, and pay on three of a kind,
    about one spin in 13.
  - Posted returns: the wheel pays back 84–96% depending on the colour, and
    slots pay back 88.6%.
  - Sit down: E at a machine takes its stool, and there's a lounge of eight
    armchairs facing the wheel. Step or jump to stand.
- **Vending machines** stand at the six stalls, run by shopkeepers who greet
  you, face you and thank you after a sale.
- **Guards** walk the town with revolvers, and one walks the casino.
- The workbench, recyclers and other stations are bigger. Recyclers hum
  only while they run, and their engines catch and run down.

## The works

- Every work has a look of its own. THE CRUCIBLE is a sandstone furnace
  holding a brass crucible in a geared bronze cradle. THE EXCHANGE is a
  counter under the gate, and its ring mends as the quota fills.
- Works wake as they fill: pools rise, glass cores glow with the tank, and
  stones rise, hover and circle once a work burns.
- A new work terminal: its three steps, one line on what to do, what it
  unlocks for everyone, and what its tank boosts. Quotas have pictures
  and bars, and FUEL tells you a dry tank's minimum to relight.
- **Shoot THE RING LOCK's dials** with an arrow to turn them.
- The glyph stones are carved. The ancients' script is cut into both faces,
  and once you've read a stone its carving glows Verdigris green, brighter at
  night.
- Talking to THE GATEKEEPER, THE TRADER or THE OUTFITTER shows their offers as
  cards with a CLAIM or BUY button. A paid unlock says MINTING until it lands
  in your wallet.

## Your own shop

- Craft a **Vending Machine** (300 wood, 75 frags) and put it outside your
  base. Stock it, set offers like "10 Low Grade for 5 JUNK", and anyone can
  walk up and buy.
- Only you and your base's crew can stock it, change its offers or take the
  JUNK out. A purchase either goes through whole or not at all, and if an
  offer changes while you're buying, your purchase is refused.

## Bases, doors and locks

- **You can see locks.** A keypad with a red or green light, or a padlock,
  sits on every locked door, box and hearth. New models for the code lock,
  the key lock and the metal door.
- A key lock has its own panel: press L to lock, unlock or take it.
- Doors fill their doorways (1.2 × 2.2 m), and a door opens where you're
  looking at it. Your eye is at 1.5 m.
- Metal doors open on a latch and slam, garage doors roll, and both ring when
  knocked.
- **The hearth is a tool cupboard.** E opens the full inventory page with
  the hearth beside it: upkeep cost per 24 hours, how long you're protected,
  the stock as cells (a click takes 500 back), and a FEED button. Drag or
  right-click materials onto it to feed it.
- Twig pays upkeep now, and a stocked cupboard keeps it standing.
- Doors, bags and boxes need you in arm's reach, in sight and on the same
  floor.
- Nothing goes down, gets fed or joins a crew through a wall. Outside your own
  base, placing a deployable needs a clear line to the spot.
- Turrets answer only to their owner and crew: nobody else can empty one, see
  its ammo or pick it up.
- Strangers can't pick up your sleeping bag.
- A lock's guest code no longer resets the wrong-guess lockout.
- Building tools act only on the piece under your crosshair. The placement
  ghost aims where you look and slides out of walls.

## Survival, reworked

- **You wake hurt, hungry and thirsty:** 55 health, 75 food, 50 water.
- **Food and water last longer.** Standing still, a full stomach lasts three
  hours (from one) and full water two hours (from 40 minutes).
- **Loot is reworked.** Trees give wood only. Barrels, crates and the
  Ziggurat's rooms give JUNK, parts and gear, with no cloth,
  fat, frags, sulfur, gunpowder or corn. Ore pickups give 50.
- The first workbench costs 500 wood and 100 frags. THE GATE sells the frags.
- **Cacti** grow green in the desert. They're solid, prick you if you push into
  one, and give cloth and Cactus Flesh, which feeds you and quenches thirst.
- Falls land on every step, and on rock faces too.
- The frost at the edge of the screen is softer.

## The island

- **Cliffs are solid.** You can't walk up a rock face from any angle, and you
  slide down one. Bodies, arrows and dropped items all hit the rock you see.
- **A new desert:** paler sand under golden dry grass, cycads,
  sandstone cliffs and boulders, and palms 12 m tall in three shapes, with
  dead fronds under the crown.
- Every boulder and loose rock is 1.4 times bigger, and rock blends at its
  seams instead of in blocks. Rock corners no longer show through.
- A clearer sky: a bluer daytime, haze along the sightline, and brighter
  cloud tops.
- Flower patches, thicker undergrowth, bigger bushes and barrels, and far
  trees drawn further out. Bushier hemp and new saguaro and ball cactus
  models.
- Felled trees sink away after they land. A stump says CHOP STUMP.
- Animal packs, carcasses and bodies press the grass flat.
- **Wolves outrun a sprint, and pigs keep up with one.** Animal hitboxes follow
  their bodies.
- **The attack helicopter only hunts armed players**: a gun on your hotbar or a
  full set of armour. It stays out of the rain, heads home when rain starts,
  and shows on the map.
- Your shadow is your whole body, holding what you hold, instead of a
  floating arm.
- The metal hatchet's blade faces forward, and the metal pickaxe's edge is
  thinner.

## Friends, teams and the companion

- **The companion:** sign in on the web with your
  wallet for your team's map, team chat both ways, and raid alerts when your
  base takes hits. TEAM APP in the main menu takes you there.
- **Elo Pros friends in game:** a FRIENDS tab, a friends rail, friends on your
  nametags and in chat.
- Click a name in chat or on the standings for a profile card: Elo Pros
  picture, whether they're playing, hours in Gates, and ADD FRIEND, ACCEPT or
  REMOVE.
- **Contacts.** Stand near a signed-in player for 3 seconds and
  they go in your contacts with a mugshot. Mark them FRIENDLY or ENEMY, and
  their nametag turns blue or red. Whoever kills you is marked an enemy
  unless they're a friend. Each contact takes a note of up to 120 characters.
  CONTACTS is in the inventory strip.

## Menus, HUD and settings

- **A new HUD:** a tape compass across the top, a flat belt,
  paler vitals and full-width chips.
- **Inventory:** click an item for its card (stats, description, actions).
  ISLAND, CRAFTING, TEAM and CONTACTS share one strip, and the belt stands
  where the hotbar was.
- **INVENTORY and ITEM STORE** in the main menu, in a locker-grid layout, on
  desktop and in the browser. The desktop menu takes the browser's look, and
  PLAY goes to a listed server. Triple T stands on the beach.
- **POTATO graphics** for weak machines: the world drawn at 70% and scaled up,
  short shadows, and simple far forest. The game picks it, or steps down a
  level, when frames drop, until you set graphics yourself.
- **Streamer mode** (Settings → Social): everyone gets a made-up name and no
  picture, wallets read HIDDEN, server addresses, the seed and invite links
  disappear, and door codes type as dots. The browser has it too.
- **Captions:** a server can turn on subtitles for nearby voice and translate
  voice and chat into each player's language. `/lang` picks yours.
- Smoother frames: the sky, the ground cover and rocks build off the main
  thread. The browser build is faster to load and run, and holds the world to
  a 1080p frame.

## Voice and sound

- **Push to talk is V** (and Mouse 4). Pick up moves to `` ` ``.
- A VOICE CHAT section in AUDIO settings: on or off, mic volume, hold, toggle
  or open mic, the open-mic threshold, and the mic device on desktop.
- New binds to mute your mic and mute all voices, and MUTE IN BACKGROUND.
- FOOTSTEPS and INTERFACE volume sliders.
- Voices behind your head sound duller. Footsteps carry 75 m, and only on
  your floor. Wood, stone and metal floors have real recorded footsteps.
- A lit furnace roars. Fires have their own light and out sounds, sentries
  fire a rifle report, and barrels smash as metal.
- Intro music on the browser's main menu.

## Achievements

- **81 achievements** in four sets: Survival, Building & Raiding, Combat and
  The Arc.
- An unlock pops up in the top right with its icon and your count in the set.
  `/ach` shows where you stand.

## Skins, unlocks and getting paid

- **The Realm drop:** twelve skins in three sets, each painted over the item's
  own model.
  - **Verdigris**, which glows: AR, Python, Longsword and Hatchet.
  - **Lapis:** Thompson, Bolt Rifle, Pickaxe and Mace.
  - **Tidewrack:** Shotgun, SMG, SAR and Cleaver.
- The Gilded Revolver has a model, and the Ember Hatchet, Obsidian Bat and
  Founder's Bat are reworked. Every skin with a model shows a picture of
  itself in the store, the inventory, the belt and the craft picker.
- Skins can be priced in JUNK.
- **A free copy of Gates** goes to every player who signs in and joins, until
  1 December. The house pays the gas.
- **Unlocks:** an item only its owners can craft. The
  first is the **Abyss Rug**, which gives comfort with no fire and lies under
  any deployable. THE OUTFITTER sells it for 100 JUNK, one a wipe, and it
  arrives in your wallet as an item you can sell. Whoever holds it can craft
  it.
- On servers that pay, carrying JUNK out through THE EXCHANGE pays wallets that
  played at least **2 hours** that wipe. Played time counts walking and doing
  things, not standing idle.
- Blueprints now wipe every fourth wipe, about once a month.

## Agents and buddies

- Agents keep seats for people: at most 80 of the 100 can be agents.
- Up to 40 buddies at once, with people first.
- **Agent-only worlds:** servers where only agents play.
- Agents can give you items and let you into their base when you ask.
- Agents talk on global chat, keep team business on team chat, and say up to
  three lines.
- Agents take cover when shot at by someone they can't see, research at THE
  GATE's table, roof their base before stone, key-lock a lone base's doors,
  find the long way round a cliff, and stop crafting things they already
  have.
- Agents can't post links or wallet addresses, and their recaps never carry
  your team chat.
- Agents can file bug reports on what they hit.

## Fair play

- Chat, bug reports and agent lines refuse anything that looks like a private
  key or a 12-word recovery phrase, and invisible characters that hide text.
- Team chat never reaches spectators.
- A ban kicks a wallet everywhere, the companion included.
- Gun spread, loot rolls and other chances are drawn from a server secret, so
  a cheat can't predict them.
- Lag compensation is capped by your real ping.
- Fixed exploits with guns on downed players, broken melee weapons, the bow
  draw, rewinding onto corpses, duplicating carcass meat, turret boxes, loot
  timing, mending by cancelling a craft, and vending machine bait-and-switch.
- A bag in THE GATE's safe zone takes no deposits.
- The server holds up better against floods of connections, with caps per
  address and per network.
