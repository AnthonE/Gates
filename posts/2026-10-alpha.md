# Safe Zone

**Patch notes · 13 October 2026**

The alpha is close. Since First Light the island has doubled in width, grown a
town in the middle and learned to tell a story across a wipe. Wolves hunt in
packs now, something patrols the sky, and you can pick a friend up off the
floor. Here is what changed.

---

## A bigger, wilder island

The island is 4 km across now: twice as wide, four times the ground.

- Two mountain ranges cut by gullies, with forest up to a treeline at 68 m.
- A desert across the north: sand, palms, cactus and dry scrub.
- Calm lakes inland. Lake water is fresh, so drinking it costs no health.
- Trees three times taller (pines reach 42 m), and cliffs made of rock, with
  crags and ledges you can stand on.
- Landmarks worth the walk: radio masts, ruined keeps, watchtowers, standing
  stones, container yards, quarries and a relay, joined to the coast road by
  dirt trails.
- The coast road is 8 m and two lanes, under power lines, with junk piles of
  wrecked cars, oil barrels, road signs and food boxes along it.

## THE GATE and the Black Ziggurat

THE GATE is a walled container town around a ruined black-stone gate near the
middle of the island, and the one safe place on it.

- No weapons drawn and no looting other players' bags. Four sentry guns on the
  watchtowers shoot anyone who turns hostile, and hostile lasts five minutes,
  death included.
- A public workbench, a research table and three recyclers that return 67%.
- Six kiosks trade JUNK for rations, building supplies, tools, parts, salvage
  and arms.
- Once you have been there, you can respawn there every 30 minutes.
- Sleepers left in town for 20 minutes are killed, so log off at home.

Out on the island, the Black Ziggurat has three sealed rooms. Green keycards
drop from caches and crates; the green room guarantees a blue card, the blue
room a red, and the sanctum pays elite loot. Every swipe wears the card.

## The wipe tells a story

Five works wake up over the course of a wipe. Each one opens for deposits at a
set hour and the whole server feeds it together. Once lit, it changes the
island for everyone, and if nobody manages it, it lights itself at a fallback
hour. Keep its tank fuelled and it gives a bonus on top.

| Work | Lit, it unlocks | Fuelled, it gives |
|---|---|---|
| THE CRUCIBLE | gunpowder (none exists before it) | furnaces smelt twice as fast |
| THE LAPIS WELLS | medkits at the rations kiosk | heals land twice as fast |
| THE OBSERVATORY | glass windows | research twice as fast |
| THE GOLDEN SPINE | garage doors | every swing at a node pays half again |
| THE EXCHANGE | JUNK out of the island to your wallet, on servers that pay out | half as much again may leave |

THE GATEKEEPER and THE TRADER talk, and what they say changes with the works.
Glyph stones teach the ancients' script, and one of them holds the answer to
THE RING LOCK, which pays a green keycard. The ISLAND screen tracks all of it,
with WORK and JOURNAL tabs.

**Standings** rank every wipe four ways: THE WORKS (what you gave and did,
which can't be raided), THE HOARD (what your base is worth at the wipe), THE
FIGHT (kills) and THE ISLAND (all of it). A hall keeps the winners after the
wipe ends, and `/top` shows the boards. On islands that pay, each board's top
places are paid to the winner's wallet.

**Wipes** count down in chat, then the island starts over. Blueprints and the
glyphs you've learned survive one wipe.

## Animals hunt back, and something flies

- Wolves hunt in packs. Two bite while the rest circle, they howl for each
  other and keep off a lit torch, and twice as many come out at night.
- Animals hear you: a gunshot carries 100 m and a satchel 200 m. Prey runs and
  wolves come to look. Crouch behind an animal and it won't see you.
- The stag joins the pig and the wolf. It only runs.
- Fel beasts: on wipe day the animals are soft and wary (60% health, half the
  bite). They harden as the works wake, up to 150% health and 140% bite.
- Butcher a carcass with a blade for meat, fat, bone and leather. Hatchets and
  spears get more out of it.
- An attack helicopter flies in from the sea for a five-minute patrol every 30
  minutes. It circles anyone it spots outside town and fires in bursts. Break
  line of sight, because it can't be shot down.

## Bows, guns and raids

- Hold right mouse to draw the bow (one second) and left click to loose. A
  body hit does 50, ×1.5 to the head, and you can fire every 1.25 s. The
  crossbow hits for 60.
- Arrows stick where they land (trunks, walls, the ground, bodies), and E pulls
  them back out. One in ten breaks.
- New arrows: high velocity, bone and fire. R picks which one you nock. A fire
  arrow burns 2 hp a second for 20–40 s where it lands.
- Right mouse on a gun aims down the sights. Guns, bows, tools and armour wear
  out, and a broken gun won't fire. You can unload a gun into your pack, and
  it switches rounds when the loaded kind runs out.
- Auto turret (a Workbench-2 blueprint): it shoots strangers using its own box
  of pistol rounds and leaves your crew alone.
- One satchel in five is a dud. It drops at the wall, and you can pick it up
  and plant it again.
- Crouch with Ctrl or Z: slower, lower, quieter and harder to hit.

## Building and bases

- New shapes on the plan: floor frames (square and triangle), half and low
  walls, foundation steps, ramps, and L, U, spiral and triangle-spiral stairs.
  The hammer turns pieces.
- New inserts: glass windows, wood shutters, metal window bars and garage
  doors.
- Deployables go wherever you aim, and R turns them. Sleeping bags go on
  foundations and floors, and fire pits go on floors.
- A code lock goes on the hearth, and whoever enters the right code joins its
  crew. E at the hearth shows its stock, the daily cost and how long the base
  is protected. Keys 1–4 take stock back out.
- Upkeep scales: bigger bases and bigger crews pay more, doors and windows
  pay their share, and a base that stops paying rots from the roof down.
- The key lock (75 wood) and the wooden barricade (200 wood, 350 hp) need no
  bench.

## Crafting, research and loot

- Put an item and some JUNK in the research table, and ten seconds later you
  get its Blueprint. Read it to learn the recipe, or trade it.
- Each workbench opens its own tier of the tech tree. A bench one level above a
  recipe crafts it in half the time, and two levels above in a quarter.
- Every container pays guaranteed JUNK (barrel 2, cache 5, crate 8), plus
  salvage (springs, pipes, gun bodies, tech trash and more) that recycles
  into more.
- The furnace, burlap, large box and code lock need no bench, and ore smelts
  inside a lit furnace. The camp fire has fuel, input and output slots and an
  on/off switch.
- Crafting and inventory have separate pages now, with colour icons, item
  descriptions and a live queue. Right-click a queued craft to make it next.
- Skins: pick a look on the craft page, from six launch skins. Ones you don't
  own show their price and open the store.

## Staying alive

- The weather cycles through six presets every 45 minutes: clear, overcast,
  fog, rain, heavy rain and storm. Storms raise the sea.
- Rain soaks you unless something is overhead. Cold nights, wind and wet
  clothes chill you until it costs health. A fire, a torch and the right
  clothes keep you warm, and the new hide poncho is the warmest thing you can
  wear.
- Pick each other up: aim at a downed player and hold E for six seconds. A
  medkit on your belt saves a failed recovery roll, and the new medical
  syringe gets a downed player back up.
- Fall damage: four metres is free, eight costs about half your health and
  fourteen kills. Deep water breaks the fall.
- Grow corn and hemp in a planter box by daylight under open sky. Carry a
  drink in a waterskin, pick berries and mushrooms, and take a second wood
  harvest from a stump.
- F lights and snuffs a held torch. A lit torch lights up the night and keeps
  wolves off.
- On the death screen, Tab or the arrow keys pick which sleeping bag you wake
  on.

## How it looks and sounds

- Meet Triple T. He's on the main menu, he's the figure on your inventory
  page wearing whatever you wear, and you wake holding his bat. It hits like
  the old rock and goes *tung*.
- The score is four tracks, with Safe Zone on the menu. Songs come with four
  to eight minutes between them and never start in the middle of a fight.
- Nearly every sound is a real recording now: footsteps, wolves, a red deer's
  bellow, axe chops, gunfire, supersonic flybys, crickets after dusk. You
  hear other players reload, eat, drink and punch in codes. Underwater,
  everything is muffled.
- A new sky and sea: weather clouds, stars, a sharp moon, and water that
  reflects the sky, turns red at dusk and breaks on the shore.
- Bodies move properly: gaits match speed, feet plant and heads turn. Other
  players are drawn in what they're wearing, and sleepers lie down.

## The browser, sign-in and agents

- The browser page is the game's main menu: server list, item store, news,
  spectating and options. If you're signed in on elopros.com, you're signed in
  here too.
- Browsers with WebGPU now get the desktop's full-range shadows and live sky.
  Everything else falls back to WebGL2 by itself.
- Guests can play the public server with no wallet. A guest plays a fresh
  character each time, so sign in to keep your base.
- Gates plays inside an X post, and an invite link opens Gates straight onto
  your server.
- Your Elo Pros name and picture show in chat, in the kill feed and on
  nametags.
- AI agents play too. They show as (agent) and talk in local chat, and you
  can watch one live from the browser.
