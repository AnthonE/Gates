# The scene can be photographed on this box, and six inhabitants is what stops it

2026-09-17. Nine frames of a populated world, seven clean plates of an empty
one, and the island map — the first frames this tree has held since capture
went dark on 2026-08-28.

## §1 · The population is the variable, and the failure is deterministic

`ci/scene.sh`'s default is `--population 6`. At six, **the probe dies at frame
85 or 86 every single run** — one frame after the world finishes building,
before its first shot. Three runs:

| run | world built | died | pieces placed | struct hits | frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | frame 84 | frame 85 | 879 | 384 | **0** |
| 2 | frame 85 | frame 86 | 873 | 390 | **0** |
| 3 | frame 85 | frame 86 | 873 | 390 | **0** |

⚠ **This was misread as flakiness first**, on the strength of `scene.sh`'s own
*"three of four runs died on one pass"* comment, and three retries were spent
on it. **The sim is deterministic** — same seed, same `dev_spawn`, same bot
schedule — so the runs are the *same world* and the counts above say so. A
deterministic failure is never retried into a success; that half-hour is the
cost of not reading its own numbers.

The mechanism follows from what `dev_spawn` is for: it puts every body on one
point so the builders and the camera are neighbours. The builders therefore
build **on top of the camera**, and half the population then raids that base
(384 structure hits) while the probe stands inside it, unarmed, for the ~70 s
the world takes to stream in.

**At `--population 2` all nine frames land**, including `7-player` and
`8-build` — one builder, one raider, 316 pieces, 128 structure hits. That is
the setting to use on a box with no GPU.

## §2 · It answers `NOW.md` §0fp item 1, which was waiting on exactly this

That item reads: *"The viewmodel is drawn at TRUE scale 0.52 m from the eye,
so a 0.6 m hatchet fills about three-quarters of the frame height… it has
never been judged against a frame with a real model in it, because until now
there was no frame."*

There are six now. The hatchet and forearm occupy roughly the lower-right
quarter of every frame in the populated set. The knob is `VIEWMODEL_HOLD` and
the call is the operator's.

## §3 · Three other things the frames show

1. **An unmodelled held item draws a pale box.** The empty-island run had
   `item.wood` in slot 0, which has no `ui::hold::HELD_MODELS` row, so a
   floating cube sits in all seven frames (`NOW.md` §0hand). For a
   photograph, put a modelled item in slot 0: `--kit item.hatchet_stone:1`.
2. **`7-player` is a picture of a wall.** The probe solved for the nearest
   body and stood flat against twig. `scene.sh` says this itself — it aims by
   arithmetic, and `--play` exists for the half a person does better.
3. **Granite reads as a smooth featureless mass** at distance (`NOW.md`
   §0rock).

## §4 · Two map readings that dissolved on inspection, which is the point

`cargo run -p client --example map_png -- 20260731 1400 out.ppm` needs no GPU.
Two things looked like defects in the thumbnail and are not:

- The straight interior road appeared to run past the coast into the sea. It
  does not — it makes a clean T into the coast ring.
- There is a ~44 m gap in the middle of it. That is at frac (0.459, 0.561),
  and the example's own stdout prints `site 2 Inland H9 … frac (0.459,
  0.561)`: **the freight depot sits on the road and the road is cleared for
  it.** It reads as broken only because this example deliberately draws none
  of the overlay — grid, labels, site names, marker badges, player arrow —
  that the real screen puts on top.

## §5 · How to repeat it

A fresh box needs `libwayland-dev libasound2-dev libudev-dev` to build
`--features render`, and `libxkbcommon-x11-0 mesa-vulkan-drivers` plus Xvfb to
run the probe (`CLAUDE.md`). The **dev** profile is enough and builds in ~10
min against release's much longer link: this workspace already compiles every
dependency and `sim-core` at `opt-level = 3`, and the frame rate under
lavapipe is ~0.86 s/frame either way, because the CPU rasteriser dominates and
not the optimisation level. ⚠ That was also guessed wrong first — the death
above was blamed on a slow debug build before the timing was read.

```sh
./ci/scene.sh --population 2 --settle 420 --kit item.hatchet_stone:1
./ci/scene.sh --population 0 --no-hud          # clean plates, no viewmodel
```
